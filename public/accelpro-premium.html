Are programmes online or in person?

Delivery mode, location, duration and schedule vary by programme. Ask about the academy that interests you for current options.

Can organisations arrange a cohort?

Yes. Share your team, capability need and preferred timeline so the academy can discuss a suitable format.

How do I choose the right academy?

Start with the professional outcome you want. If more than one academy appears relevant, book a short fit conversation.
